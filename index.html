Who I am

I’m Mariam Fatima Kewang, born on June 29, 2008, and raised in the scenic town of Tadian, Mountain Province. I completed my elementary education in Balaoa, Tadian, and finished secondary school at the Tadian School of Arts and Trades. Rooted in the rich heritage and values of my hometown, I carry a strong work ethic and deep appreciation for community into everything I do.

Currently, I am an aspiring Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at the University of Cordilleras (UC). Driven by a curiosity for how systems work and how technology can solve real-world problems, I am continuously building my skills in web development, programming, and software engineering with the goal of creating clean, impactful digital solutions.
